# Assets for 3b1b/videos on ManimGL

Images, SVGs, sounds, 3D models and fonts that the scenes of the repository
[`animations_3b1b`](https://github.com/zavden/animations_3b1b) read. Clone it inside `animations_3b1b/` (as
`animations_3b1b/assets_3b1b/`) or next to it (`../assets_3b1b`); any folder given through the `THREEB1B_ASSETS`
variable also works.

The scenes run on the ManimGL fork [`manimgl_3b1b`](https://github.com/zavden/manimgl_3b1b), and their rendered videos
are on [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1BCGrsZvolya2Jwur21eW9Irj0JhkhujV?usp=sharing).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Download the videos one folder at a time, year by year** (`2017/`, `2018/`, …, `outside_videos/`), not the whole `renders_3b1b/` folder at once.
> If you download everything together, **Google Drive splits it into blocks of 1.3-1.6 GB and mixes the videos of different years inside them.**

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `PiCreature/` | The original Π character (3Blue1Brown's pi creatures are not redistributed) |
| `svg_images/`, `raster_images/` | Illustrations, icons and photos |
| `sounds/` | Sound effects |
| `models/` | 3D models |
| `fonts/` | Typefaces (SIL OFL, with their license next to them) |
| `generated/` | The scripts that created the placeholders and downloaded the photos, with their record of author, license and URL (`*_sources.json`) |

## What is and is not here

- **There is no file from the `assets/` folder that Grant removed from 3b1b/videos.** What came from there was
  replaced by original material or downloaded from a free source.
- There is work by 3Blue1Brown that is still published in 3b1b/videos (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), public-domain material, CC0,
  CC BY and CC BY-SA, SIL OFL fonts and original placeholders.
- Each third-party file **keeps its own license**. The author, license and source of each one are in
  `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
- The rest (the original placeholders and the selection) is distributed under
  [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) (see `LICENSE`).
